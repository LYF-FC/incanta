# Decisions

## Butler supersedes Incanta
Incanta remains a sandbox/reference project.

## No parallel production platform
Do not evolve Incanta into a second competing household assistant.

## Reuse selectively
Reuse components only where they are clearly better than rebuilding.

## Local-first experimentation
Private AI experiments should remain local by default.

## Archive aggressively
Once a component is superseded and no longer useful for experiments, archive it.
